## ✍️ Chapter I — About the Author

Hi, I'm **Mustafijur Rahman** 👋 — a book formatter and cover designer from Bangladesh.

Since **2017** I've been helping self-published authors turn finished manuscripts into professional, publish-ready books. Print or digital, fiction or non-fiction, a 40-page picture book or a 400-page novel — my job is to make your book look like it came from a real publishing house, and to make sure it passes every platform check on the first upload.

- 📚 **2,500+ books** formatted for **1,000+ authors** worldwide
- 🌍 Based in **Bangladesh**, working with authors in every time zone
- 🗣️ Speaks **English** & **Bengali**
- ⚡ Usually replies within **about an hour**
- 💼 Full-time freelance book formatter & cover designer since **December 2017**
